Why computers matter

A powerful tool for a better world

Computers make information, creativity and opportunities more accessible. Here are some of the most important ways they improve our lives.

🎓

Education

Students can attend online classes, research topics, practise skills and access digital libraries from almost anywhere.

💬

Communication

Email, messaging and video calls help people share ideas and stay connected across long distances.

🏥

Healthcare

Hospitals use computers to organise records, support medical research and help professionals analyse information.

🏢

Business

Businesses use computers for accounting, planning, designing, managing information and serving customers.

🔬

Science & research

Researchers use powerful computers to study data, create simulations and investigate complex questions.

🎨

Creativity & entertainment

People create art, music, films, games and animations, while enjoying a wide range of digital entertainment.

Beyond the screen

Use technology wisely

Make the most of computers

Technology is most helpful when we use it thoughtfully and responsibly.

🔐 Stay safe online

Protect personal information, use strong passwords and think before clicking unfamiliar links.

⏱️ Keep a healthy balance

Take regular breaks and make time for learning, exercise, hobbies and people offline.

🧠 Think critically

Check information from reliable sources and use digital tools to support your own thinking.
